# Changelog

## 3.1.1
- Mac: the shoot house planner's text now shows, so you can save your house.
- Map is sharp again.

## 3.1.0
- New: map card, bottom left. A live scan of everything around you (walls, fences, indoors and outdoors), centered on you and following you, with real doors marked in amber and your teammates. Enemies your recon drone marks show as red dots. Replaces the JTAC radar while on.
- New: CQB playbook (double-click Mouse 4, or any key you pick, double-click or one press): every stack, room entry and movement play animated, with door options. See-through, so the game stays visible. One click or Esc closes it.
- New: drill house in the Operation Room shoot house, installed by the mod: single walls, in through a main door on the terminal side into a U corridor (180 check), out at the far corner, straight, diagonal and L corridors, a T intersection, box, L-shaped, linear and irregular rooms, real doors that open into rooms (never into a corridor) and open doorways, enemies and flipping steel targets. Save a layout in the planner's slot 1 and it loads every time you start the game.
- Doorways: first person as you reach a door, not only once you are in it, and a faster switch, so the camera no longer cuts through your head. It holds 1 s instead of 1.5 s.
- Reload speed default 250%.
- Settings: cleaner menu, removed the teammates glass look.
- Mic: your own name no longer shows. The MIC sign is off by default (the game has its own).
- Fixed small stutters every few seconds caused by the mic and map player searches.
- Fixed blank text in other mods' screens (like the shoot house planner) by giving the game's on-screen text a font that draws.
